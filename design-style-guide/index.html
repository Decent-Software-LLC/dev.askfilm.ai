Askfilm.ai · Product design exploration

Design Options

A coach-first interface for importing full games, reviewing AI-suggested moments, validating evidence, understanding players and opponents, and assembling a film session.

Concept v0.2 · Basketball first

Vision boards

Two viable personalities using the same product principles: evidence over spectacle, speed over configuration, and the video always stays central.

Recommended palette

Arena Intelligence as the application shell, with paper-toned surfaces reserved for printable scouting and shared player reports.

Type directionCondensed display face for game/team titles; highly legible grotesk for controls and long scouting reports. Keep numerical data tabular.
Interaction characterKeyboard-first, restrained motion, instant visual feedback. AI suggestions feel provisional until a coach accepts them.
Core visual languageFilm strips, timeline markers, court geometry, evidence links, and coaching-marker strokes—never generic “AI sparkle.”

Typeface examples

Both systems use a practical sans-serif for controls. Their display faces create two very different brand personalities.
